BLACKJACK — C++17

READY-TO-PLAY COPY (no compiler or installation needed)
Extract the entire play folder before starting.
Windows 10/11 x64: double-click simple.exe.
macOS 11 or newer, Intel or Apple Silicon: double-click Start Blackjack.command.
Linux x64: open a terminal in the folder and run ./blackjack.
The play folder contains separate executables for these systems.

BUILDING THE SOURCE COPY
You need an installed compiler supporting C++17. No package manager or network
access is used by these builds. FTXUI is already in third_party/ftxui.
Linux/student cluster or macOS: sh build.sh, then ./blackjack.
Windows with MinGW-w64 g++ available: build.bat, then double-click simple.exe.
Optional CMake: cmake -S . -B build, then cmake --build build --config Release.

PLAYING
Enter your name and press Enter. Enter a whole-dollar bet from $1 to your balance.
You start with $100. Wins pay even money; ties return your bet. Aces count as
11 or 1 to avoid a bust. The dealer draws below 17 and stands on 17 or higher.
During your turn, press a number or select a button with Tab/arrows and Enter:
  1 Stand       2 Draw front     3 Draw back
  4 Peek front  5 Peek back      6 Shuffle
Peeks remain visible until the deck changes. Drawing removes a card from the
chosen end of the main deque. Shuffling reorders that same deque. An exhausted
deck is refilled automatically. Choose Play again after a round, or Quit.
Q quits after entering your name; Esc quits at any time, including animations.
Resize your terminal for more room. Long hands scroll to show the latest card.
Hearts and diamonds are red; other text uses your terminal's default foreground.
Set NO_COLOR to disable suit colours.
